1. **Research foundation (this pass):** source index, all 103 main numbers, 37 Free Mission catalog identities, contemporary side-mission links, entity census, contradictions and automated validation.
2. **Pure engine foundation (begun after research):** strict schemas, typed conditions/events, asset resolver, deterministic gauges, navigation and maze graph, transaction-safe versioned storage. Synthetic fixtures are explicitly RESTORATION.
3. **Episode 0 vertical slice:** strongest simple early narrative. Trace George → Skoll → school tour → Morris. First obtain full screenshots, map geometry, exact triggers and reward/rank transition. A retold dialogue script must be labeled RECONSTRUCTED/RESTORATION, never quoted as original.
4. **Episode 1 combat/pet slice:** Mina → Julia/Morris → home village with Shou → Samuel's wand → Kesno duel. Research all three homeland branches, pet award timing, battle rules and rank terminology first.
5. **Episode 2:** reconstruct school basement excursion and Odangka/forest chain, Baby Eagle timing, Isaac companion and actual encounter groups. Do not compress into a generic talk/fight/reward loop.
6. **Episode 4 and school services:** typed phrase, resin powder and crescent door provide a concrete interaction target; verify input mechanism, faction-specific scroll award and lesson requirements. Classes/shops/pet care depend on transaction and progression systems.
7. **Episodes 6–8:** do the first authentic maze only after tracing every room edge and clue from surviving references. The narrative alone does not recover the graph. Preserve backtracking and repeated Guillaume encounters.
8. **Episodes 3, 5, 9–20, then arc-by-arc expansion:** work from the linked dossier with map/asset/encounter dependencies. Maintain completion milestones independently. Prioritize contemporary sources where accessible.
9. **Free Missions:** establish basic promotion exams, pet contests, teleport unlocks and spirit trials; then remaining errands, repeatables and seasonal content. Do not merge overlapping spirit quests without evidence.
10. **Public phase:** finish licensed replacement art/audio, server-validated economy, two-user RLS tests and auth flow; choose `/game/` hosting; run artifact audit. Dorm/social/fishing/PvP follow stable single-player systems.
