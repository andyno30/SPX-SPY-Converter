# Monsters

Research pass: 2026-09-07. Coverage is partial; unknowns are intentional.

The registry extends beyond the brief to a broader list of Earth, Ice and Flame monsters. The source's elemental section grouping is **RECONSTRUCTED / MEDIUM**, not an extracted monster database. [Uman monster catalog](https://tcatmon.com/wiki/아르피아/몬스터).

Original HP, MP, level, skills, location variants, drops, EXP and Pin remain unknown. Anecdotal HP statements are not used as runtime defaults. Historical patches may have changed encounters. The fire affiliation of Wood Chunk is counterintuitive and must not be changed based on appearance.

Rainbow Worm / 무지개 지렁이 is linked to Episode 8's narrative but has no authenticated stat block. Different labels for octopus, squid, logs and scorpions must be resolved before merging IDs. Asset IDs are requirements, not evidence of recovered or licensed sprites.
