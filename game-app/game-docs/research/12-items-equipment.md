Research pass: 2026-09-07. Coverage is partial; unknowns are intentional.

The promotion account discusses Mana Stone collection, but its author's approximate 90% observation is not a measured drop rate and commenters dispute it. No probability is adopted. [Promotion account](https://alicer.tistory.com/59).

Equipment slots, element/rank eligibility, stat bonuses, cosmetic overlays, shop stock and sell prices need screenshot extraction. Never infer a quantity of zero from a blank field. Consumables, quest-bound items and equipment should be distinct runtime item types; currency is stored separately.
