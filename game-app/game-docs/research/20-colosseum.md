# Colosseum

Research pass: 2026-09-07. Coverage is partial; unknowns are intentional.

School Colosseum, Wizard City Colosseum and Kingdom Arena are distinct names in the map source. Do not merge them. [Map catalog](https://lostmediawiki.kr/w/마법학교%20아르피아/맵).

A contemporary promotion account includes an arena battle and outcome. This is direct visual evidence for that battle presentation, not the full PvP ruleset. [Have Alice](https://alicer.tistory.com/59).

Free Mission pet competitions are cataloged. Eligibility, enemy sets, prizes, scoring and repeatability remain unknown. The battle model separates allied/enemy teams and controller ownership; real-time PvP is deferred until deterministic single-player rules and authenticated rewards are stable.
