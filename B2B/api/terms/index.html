7 Data freshness and technical limitations

The API reads a shared server-side snapshot generated from the existing backend cache. Customer requests do not trigger Yahoo Finance or other upstream quote requests. The existing updater schedule and upstream request budget remain independent of API traffic. Snapshot copy time does not reset the age of its source data.

The service applies a 120-second maximum source-cache age. A missing or expired source snapshot returns an unavailable response instead of being represented as current. This cache policy does not guarantee that the underlying exchange quote is less than 120 seconds old: markets may be closed and providers may publish delayed quotes. Quote timestamps, source timestamps and available delay information must be considered together. No quote is represented as real-time unless the relevant provider and licensing support that description.

Conversion results can be affected by delayed, missing, stale or erroneous inputs, instrument specifications, contract selection, rounding, market discontinuities and outages. Null fields and unavailable instruments must be handled by Customer's application. Provider does not guarantee accuracy, uninterrupted availability, trading execution, profitability, or fitness for an individual trading decision. Customer must independently verify information and implement risk controls before trading or relying on the output.
