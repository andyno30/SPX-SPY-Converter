SPYCONVERTER API / LEGAL

Proposed API License Agreement

Draft for qualified legal review · version 2026-10-15-draft

Not an active agreement. This proposal requires qualified legal review, confirmed market-data rights, completed legal-entity details, and an approved electronic signature workflow before activation. The local simulator has no legal effect.

1. Parties and scope

This proposed agreement is between the legal operator of SpyConverter.com (legal entity and address to be completed by counsel) and the customer identified in the executed order form. It governs authorized API access only; existing SpyConverter Pro subscriptions remain separate.

2. Authorized internal applications

Subject to an executed agreement and payment, Developer permits one internal application; Pro permits one commercial internal application; Business permits multiple approved internal applications and requires manual review. Access is nonexclusive, nontransferable, and limited to the documented endpoints and approved use.

3. Restrictions

Customers may not resell, redistribute, publicly display, sublicense, or otherwise supply API data or access to third parties without prior written authorization and all required provider rights. Unauthorized scraping, key sharing outside the approved organization, attempts to evade limits, and attempts to access another customer’s data are prohibited.

4. Market-data and third-party rights

SpyConverter’s calculation engine is distinct from upstream market inputs. Calculating a ratio does not establish redistribution rights. Availability is subject to supplier and exchange permissions, attribution, delay, storage, use and redistribution restrictions. Yahoo-derived data remains disabled until authorization is confirmed. Customer-supplied data would require a separately reviewed capability and sufficient rights.

5. Accuracy, freshness and trading decisions

Quotes and calculated values can be delayed, stale, missing or incorrect. Cache publication time does not guarantee quote freshness. The API is not investment advice, an execution service, or a guarantee of trading results. Customers must validate suitability and independently manage their trading decisions and risks.

6. Availability

No uptime, uninterrupted access, execution, or latency guarantee is offered unless stated in a separately executed service agreement. Access may be interrupted for maintenance, provider failures, security incidents, or licensing restrictions. Stale source snapshots are rejected after the documented freshness window.

7. Fees, quotas and cancellations

Monthly prices are USD $25 Developer, $50 Pro, and $150 Business. Monthly quotas are respectively 10,000, 100,000, and 500,000 requests; minute limits are 10, 30, and 120. Quotas follow the paid Stripe billing period. No automatic overage fees, free commercial API tier, unlimited plan, or annual plan is offered. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period. Applicable taxes and any legally required consumer rights must be addressed in the final agreement.

8. Payment failures, refunds and upgrades

Access requires a verified paid entitlement. Failed payments suspend access pending successful reconciliation. Refunds and disputes suspend access pending review; the final refund policy requires legal approval. Customers may request upgrades. Changes are subject to approval and confirmed billing terms, with no automatic usage overage charges.

9. Security and fair usage

Customers must protect API keys, restrict them to approved applications, notify support of compromise, and rotate or revoke affected credentials. Limits are applied across organization credentials. SpyConverter may investigate abusive or unauthorized use and apply proportionate restrictions consistent with applicable law.

10. Suspension and termination

Access may be suspended for nonpayment, security risks, misuse, legal requirements, or loss of upstream rights. The final agreement must specify notice, cure periods, termination rights, data retention, and any required refunds. On termination, customers must stop access and comply with provider-specific retention and deletion obligations.

11. Liability and dispute resolution

Counsel must complete permitted warranty exclusions, liability caps, excluded damages, non-excludable statutory rights, governing law, jurisdiction, and dispute procedures. No jurisdiction or enforceable limitation is asserted by this draft.

12. Electronic signature and records

Activation requires a completed workflow with an approved provider such as DocuSign or Adobe Acrobat Sign. Preserve the agreement version, verified signing identity, provider envelope ID, signing timestamp, completed document and its cryptographic digest. A checkbox or local simulation is not a substitute for that process. Business review is a separate approval.

13. Contact

API applications, support and security reports: andyno30@gmail.com. Business includes priority support; response-time commitments require a separate written agreement.
